Help & User Guide

Step-by-step instructions for using the system

👩‍💼 Receptionist Guide

Role: Receptionists manage patient registrations, appointment bookings, and billing.

Step 1 – Login

Go to the login page. Enter your email and password. After successful login you will be redirected to your Receptionist Dashboard.

Default test: reception@sunrisedental.com / reception123

Step 2 – Register a New Appointment

  1. Click New Appointment from dashboard or top menu.
  2. Search by NIC – enter the patient's NIC and click Search.
  3. If patient exists → their details are auto-filled. If not → fill in the new patient form below.
  4. Select Treatment Type (price preview shown automatically).
  5. Select Dentist, Date (future dates only), and Time (08:00–18:00).
  6. Enter Contact Number (10 digits starting with 0).
  7. Click Register Appointment → system validates and assigns a unique appointment number like SDC-2026-0001.

Step 3 – View & Search Appointments

  1. Go to Appointments from the nav menu.
  2. All appointments are listed in the table. Use the Status filter or Date filter to narrow results.
  3. Enter an appointment number (e.g. SDC-2026-0001) in the search box and click Search to view full details.
  4. From the detail panel, you can Update Status or Generate Bill.

Step 4 – Generate a Bill

  1. Go to Billing from the nav menu.
  2. Enter the appointment number and click Find – appointment details are shown.
  3. Select Payment Method (Cash / Card / Insurance).
  4. Click Generate Bill – the system calculates fees automatically:
    • Consultation Fee + Treatment Fee
    • Senior Citizen Discount (10% for patients 60+)
    • 5% Tax
  5. Click Mark as PAID when payment is received.
  6. Click Print to print the receipt.

Step 5 – Patient Records

  1. Go to Patients to see all registered patients.
  2. Use the search box to filter by name, NIC or contact.
  3. Click Edit to update patient details.
  4. Click History to see all appointments for that patient.
  5. Click Add New Patient to register a new patient without an appointment.

🦷 Dentist Guide

Dashboard

Your dashboard shows today's patient count, completed count, and remaining patients.

Today's Patients

The bottom section shows all of today's patients sorted by time. Click Done to mark an appointment as Completed.

Patient History

Go to Patient History, search by patient ID or NIC to see all past treatments for that patient, useful for ongoing treatment plans.

Schedule View

Go to My Schedule and select a date to view appointments. You can filter by date and dentist.

🛠️ Admin Guide

Role: Admins have full access including staff management, dentist management, and analytics reports.

Staff Management

Go to Staff. View all staff. Click Add Staff to create a new user. Use Deactivate to disable login without deleting.

Dentist Management

Go to Dentists. Each dentist appears as a card. Click Edit to update details. Click Add Dentist to register a new dentist.

Go to Reports to see:

  • Monthly revenue total

  • Payment method breakdown
  • Printable today's report

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The login page says "Invalid credentials"?

Ensure the backend server is running on port 8080. Check your email and password. Contact the admin to reset your password.

Q: Can I book an appointment in the past?

No. The system only allows future dates for new appointments.

Q: What if a patient is a senior citizen?

If the patient's date of birth shows they are 60 or older, a 10% senior citizen discount is automatically applied when generating the bill.

Q: What does the appointment number look like?

Format: SDC-YYYY-NNNN e.g. SDC-2026-0001

Q: What are the clinic operating hours?

Appointments can only be booked between 08:00 and 18:00. The system automatically blocks times outside clinic hours.

Q: Can the same dentist have two appointments at the same time?

No. The system detects and prevents double bookings for the same dentist at the same date and time.

Q: How do I exit the system safely?

Click Logout in the navigation bar. This clears your session. Then you may close the browser tab.
