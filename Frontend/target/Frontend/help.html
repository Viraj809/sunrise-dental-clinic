Help & User Guide

How to Use the System

Step 1: Login

Use your email and password to log in. Contact the administrator if you need credentials.

Step 2: Dashboard

After logging in, you will see your role-based dashboard with quick links to common tasks.

Step 3: Register Appointment

Go to Register Appointment, fill in patient details, select a dentist and treatment type, choose a date and time, then submit. The system validates the contact number, date, time, and prevents double bookings automatically.

Step 4: View Appointment

Search for an appointment by its appointment number (e.g., SDC-2026-0001) to see full details.

Step 5: Generate Bill

Enter the appointment ID and patient ID to generate a bill. The system calculates consultation fee, treatment fee, applicable discounts (senior citizens get 10% off), and tax.

Step 6: Reports

Admins can view daily appointment reports, revenue trends, treatment popularity, and dentist workload.
